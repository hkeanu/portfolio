## Infrastructure used day to day

The personal media stack includes Jellyfin for video libraries and Audiobookshelf for audiobooks and podcasts. It is a practical part of the homelab: services with storage, permissions, and maintenance needs that recur over time.

## Storage has its own role

Application configuration is kept separate from the media library. Containers mount the directories they need, and Jellyfin’s documented library mounts are read-only.

That separation makes it easier to reason about replacing an application container without confusing its configuration with the larger collection it reads.

## What this helps me explore

Self-hosting everyday services creates opportunities to understand filesystem permissions, container mounts, hardware acceleration, and storage planning in context.

This page focuses on those infrastructure concerns. Personal library contents and service administration interfaces are not part of the public portfolio.
